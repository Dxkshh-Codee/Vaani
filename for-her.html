Chapter 07

A letter I never knew
how to say.

Not demanding anything. Just… finally saying it honestly.

A letter I never sent

written slowly, over months —

Hey,

I don't really know how to start this without sounding like I'm trying too hard. So I'll just be honest.

I miss you. Not in the loud, dramatic way. In the quiet, everyday way — like reaching for something that used to be there without thinking.

I think about us a lot. Not just the good days, but all of it. The laughs, the late talks, the small things that felt so ordinary then and feel so precious now. And also the parts where I didn't show up the way I should have.

There are things I wish I had done differently. Times I was too stubborn, too careless with your feelings, too busy defending myself to really hear you. I’ve had a lot of time to sit with that. Not to blame myself endlessly — but to understand it. And to want to be better.

When you said you feel like you can't be fixed right now, I heard you. I didn't argue in my head. I just... felt it. I know you need your own time, your own space, your own way back to yourself. And I respect that more than I can say. You don't owe me closeness while you're trying to find your steadiness again.

I can't heal you. I know that now. I can only work on me — become calmer, kinder, more patient. More someone you'd feel safe with, if life ever lets us try again. Not performing change, just actually doing it, quietly.

I'm not writing this to ask you to come back. I'm not expecting a reply, and I don't want you to feel any pressure from this. I just wanted you to know that you mattered to me — deeply — and that a part of me still cares in that soft, steady way that doesn't demand anything.

Whatever you're doing now, wherever you are, I hope life is treating you gently. I hope you're sleeping well, laughing honestly, and giving yourself the grace you always gave others. You deserve that.

If our story is done, then I'll carry it with gratitude. If someday, somewhere down the line, we both feel ready to see what we could be now — I'd be open to that with all my heart. No expectations. No keeping score. Just two people meeting again, a little wiser.

Until then, no pressure. No waiting that asks anything of you. Just… me, here, wishing you well and remembering us kindly.

Always wishing you well, Take care of yourself ♡ — D
